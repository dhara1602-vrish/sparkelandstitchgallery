Handcrafted Detail
Artisan Craftsmanship

The Beauty Is in the Details.

♦

From intricate hand embroidery to creative stitch work and distinctive fabric designs, every detail adds character to your creation.

At Sparkle & Stitch Gallery, we celebrate the rich legacy of Indian hand needlework. Each embellishment—from fine gold zardozi wire and delicate pearl accents to vibrant threadwork and mirror placing—is placed with care to bring depth and distinction to your garments.

Zardozi & Metallic Threadwork: Royal metallic wire and dabka motifs for bridal and reception outfits.
Aari & Resham Work: Delicate silk thread floral patterns tailored to match saree and lehenga borders.
Mirror Work & Abhala Craft: Authentic Gujarati mirror embellishments perfect for festive Navratri chaniya cholis.
Custom Neckline & Border Detailing: Personalized sleeve cuffs, dori tassels, and latkan finishing.
