About Sparkle & Stitch Gallery

♦

Sparkle & Stitch Gallery is a fashion design studio specializing in custom stitching and designer women's wear, located in Samarth Complex on Gotri Road, Vadodara.

We create elegant blouses, suits, lehengas, chaniya cholis, Anarkalis, and customized outfits with quality fabrics, fine craftsmanship, and perfect fitting for weddings, festivals, parties, and everyday wear.

Every garment is shaped around the individual: honoring your preferred silhouette, neckline comfort, embroidery tastes, and celebration deadlines. Whether you bring your own beloved fabric or wish to design a distinctive piece from scratch, our studio ensures an attentive, personalized tailoring experience.

Personalized Fit

Accurate pattern drafting and structured fitting for total comfort and posture.

Fine Craftsmanship

Delicate seam finishes, secure lining, reinforced borders, and artistic hand touches.

Festive & Occasion Wear

Bridal lehengas, Navratri chaniya cholis, festive reception suits, and party styles.
